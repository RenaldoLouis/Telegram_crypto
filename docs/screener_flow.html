Crypto Screener Flow

How one 4-hour candle close becomes a Telegram brief, a scored paper trade and a Monday review. Every step runs on GitHub, so the laptop is not needed. Paper only: the system never places an order.

The flow, step by step

Steps 1 to 7 run at every 4h close. Step 8 runs once a day, step 9 once a week.

1

Get the data from Bybit

every 4h close + 2 min · public API, no key
all USDT perps (~800)→ top 100 by 24h volume→ drop < $10M volume or < $50M open interest→ top 30 · about 24 pass

Coins flagged by the momentum pulse (big move, volume spike, extreme funding) are added, and BTC, ETH and SOL are always in. For each coin it downloads finished candles on four timeframes: 15m × 100, 1h × 300, 4h × 300, 1D × 210. From those it computes RSI, EMA 20/50, ATR, ADX, MACD, volume spike and the 20-candle range. About 100 API calls per scan.

2

Check the six rules

on the last finished candle · signal_rules.py

Each rule looks for one chart pattern. A signal only counts for 90 minutes after its 4h candle closes (45 minutes on the 1h), because the trade is meant to start right after the close. See the six rules.

3

Build the trade

mechanical_setups.py
limit order at the next 15m open · 30 min to fill stop from the rule T1 0.75R: close half, stop to breakeven after +1R: stop to +0.3R T2 1.5–2R

R is the distance from entry to stop. Worked example of one trade.

4

Safety checks

main.py

The levels must make sense (T1 no further than 1R, T2 at least 1.5R). One setup per coin per scan, and no repeat of a coin that already had a setup in the last 2 days.

5

Sort into lanes

by each rule's backtest record
ExecuteEmptyNeeds ≥ 70% profitable on ≥ 30 test trades. No rule has reached it.
Watch · shownRange top fadeNeeds ≥ 60% on ≥ 30 test trades. Shown in the brief and paper-tracked.
Shadow · not shownThe other fiveLogged and scored, so their live record keeps growing.
6

Daily book: up to 3 picks a day

paper trial 19 Oct → 1 Nov

Picks come from the watch and shadow lanes, first come first served across the day (UTC). Within one scan the order is fixed by backtest record, one pick per coin per day, longs and shorts both allowed.

7

Telegram brief

morning brief always · 07:05 WIB

BTC first: price, daily and 4h trend, RSI and ADX, volume against its 20-day average, funding, open interest, yesterday's high and low, market breadth, and who got liquidated. Then the day's picks with entry, stop and targets. Other scans only send a message when they make a pick.

8

Score the trades

daily after 00:00 UTC · weekly_eval.py

Once a pick's window has run out (2 days for 4h signals, 1 day for 1h), the trade is replayed on 15m candles with the same simulator the backtests use: fill, stop, T1, breakeven, T2, fees and funding. Each pick gets a net R and a profitable yes or no.

9

Monday review

Telegram + file · weekly_review.py

The week's picks, win rate and net R, longs against shorts, each setup, and the trial so far. A side or setup with 20 or more scored picks and net below zero is flagged to cut; you make the change with one line in the config. It ends with the carry line: what holding spot against a short perp or quarterly future pays right now.

Running in the background

  • Momentum pulse, every 2 hours: flags fast-moving coins for step 1 and labels the market (risk-off, cautious, neutral, risk-on).
  • Liquidation feed: a live connection to Bybit that runs alongside every scan job, summarised for the brief.
  • Weekly momentum book, Mondays: the separate paper test that buys the strongest coins and shorts the weakest.
  • Health alert: a Telegram message if any 4h close is missed. Every close since 12 Oct was caught on the first try.
  • Monthly facts file, every 30 days: each test judged by its own rule. Next one around 15 Nov.

How one trade is managed

A worked example of a long. Each level is measured in R, the distance from entry to stop. Shorts are the mirror image.

  1. Price reaches T1 at +0.75R: half the position is closed and the stop on the rest moves to the entry price, so the trade can no longer lose.
  2. Price reaches +1R: the stop moves up to +0.3R to lock part of the gain.
  3. Price turns back down and hits that stop: the second half closes at +0.3R. Had it kept going, the second half would have closed at T2 (+2R here).

½ × 0.75R + ½ × 0.3R = +0.525R before fees, about +0.48R after fees and funding. Any trade that ends above zero after costs counts as profitable.
