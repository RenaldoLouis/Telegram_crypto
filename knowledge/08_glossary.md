# Glossary — Terms Claude Will Use

Quick reference for terms appearing across the knowledge files and in briefs.

---

## Price Action / Structure

- **BOS (Break of Structure)**: price breaks a significant swing high/low in the direction of the trend. Continuation signal.
- **ChoCH (Change of Character)**: price breaks a swing level against the current trend. First sign of reversal.
- **Order Block (OB)**: last opposite-color candle before a strong move; often acts as future S/R.
- **Fair Value Gap (FVG) / Imbalance**: three-candle inefficiency; price tends to return to fill it.
- **Liquidity Sweep / Stop Hunt**: wick beyond an obvious S/R level to trigger stops, then reversal.
- **Spring** (Wyckoff): false breakdown below a range, followed by reclaim — bullish reversal.
- **Upthrust** (Wyckoff): false breakout above a range, followed by breakdown — bearish reversal.
- **Last Point of Support (LPS)**: retest of broken resistance as new support — classic Wyckoff entry.

## Wyckoff Phases

- **Accumulation**: smart money buying from weak hands at lows. Flat price, declining volume.
- **Markup**: trending up phase after accumulation completes.
- **Distribution**: smart money selling to retail at highs. Flat price, high volume.
- **Markdown**: trending down phase after distribution completes.

## Volume / Order Flow

- **Relative Volume (RVOL)**: current volume / N-period average. Normalizes for context.
- **Delta**: buying volume minus selling volume in a period.
- **CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta)**: running total of delta. Divergences are meaningful.
- **Absorption**: high volume with little price movement — a big player absorbing flow.
- **Climactic Volume**: massive spike often marking exhaustion / turning points.
- **POC (Point of Control)**: price with highest volume in a period. Major reference level.
- **Value Area (VA)**: price range containing 70% of volume. Fair value zone.
- **High Volume Node (HVN)**: price zone with concentrated volume. S/R magnet.
- **Low Volume Node (LVN)**: price zone with minimal volume. Price moves fast through these.

## Derivatives / Perpetuals

- **Funding Rate**: periodic payment between longs and shorts to keep perp price near spot.
  - Positive: longs pay shorts.
  - Negative: shorts pay longs.
- **Open Interest (OI)**: total notional of open positions. Rising OI = new money entering.
- **Mark Price**: weighted index used for liquidations (not exchange last price).
- **Basis**: difference between perp price and spot price.
- **Contango**: perp trading above spot (bullish positioning).
- **Backwardation**: perp trading below spot (bearish or fear positioning).
- **Isolated Margin**: position has its own margin; limited downside.
- **Cross Margin**: positions share account margin; one liquidation can cascade.
- **ADL (Auto-Deleveraging)**: force-closing of profitable positions when insurance fund depleted. Rare.

## Risk / Position Sizing

- **R (Risk Unit)**: the dollar amount risked per trade. 1R = your max loss if stop hits.
- **R:R (Reward-to-Risk Ratio)**: target distance / stop distance. Minimum acceptable: 2:1.
- **ATR (Average True Range)**: average range of N recent candles; used for volatility-based stops.
- **Kelly Criterion**: formula for optimal position size given win rate and payoff. Use quarter-Kelly at most.
- **Fixed Fractional**: risk a constant % of account per trade (e.g., 1%).
- **Drawdown**: peak-to-trough decline in account value.
- **Max Adverse Excursion (MAE)**: how far against you a trade went before working or stopping out.

## Crypto-Specific

- **BTC.D (Bitcoin Dominance)**: BTC market cap as % of total crypto market cap. Risk indicator for alts.
- **Altseason**: period when alts significantly outperform BTC. Typically falling BTC.D + rising BTC.
- **Flippening**: hypothetical event where ETH market cap exceeds BTC. Has not occurred.
- **CME Gap**: weekend gap between CME futures close and spot trading. ~70-80% fill historically.
- **Liquidation Cascade**: chain of forced closures that amplifies a move.
- **Halving**: quadrennial event reducing BTC block reward. Core of the 4-year cycle thesis.

## Trading Psychology

- **FOMO**: Fear Of Missing Out — chasing extended moves.
- **FUD**: Fear, Uncertainty, Doubt — negative sentiment (often narrative-driven).
- **Tilt**: emotional compromise of decision-making after losses or stress.
- **Revenge Trade**: entering a trade to "make back" a loss rather than because setup qualifies.
- **Confirmation Bias**: only seeking information that supports existing view.
- **Anchoring**: fixating on entry price or prior highs/lows as reference for decisions.
- **Thinking in Probabilities** (Douglas): mental framework treating every trade as one sample from a distribution.

## Indicators (common ones referenced)

- **RSI (14)**: Relative Strength Index. 0-100 oscillator. > 70 overbought, < 30 oversold.
- **EMA (20, 50, 200)**: Exponential Moving Average. Trend indicators, dynamic S/R.
- **MACD**: Moving Average Convergence Divergence. Momentum + trend.
- **Bollinger Bands**: volatility envelope around moving average.
- **ATR**: volatility-based range indicator (also used for stops).
- **OBV (On-Balance Volume)**: cumulative volume indicator for divergence spotting.

## Analytical Tools / Data Sources

- **Coinglass**: aggregated liquidation data, funding rates, OI heatmaps.
- **CryptoQuant**: on-chain + exchange flows.
- **Glassnode**: on-chain metrics, holder behavior.
- **TradingView**: charting platform (primary tool for retail TA).
- **Bookmap / ATAS / Cignals**: professional order flow tools.
- **Messari / Delphi**: research platforms.

## Setup Names (from Playbook)

- **Trend Pullback**: entering in direction of HTF trend at a pullback level.
- **Range Breakout**: entering on breakout of consolidation, preferably on retest.
- **Spring Reversal**: buying a Wyckoff spring at range lows.
- **Liquidity Sweep Reversal**: fading a stop hunt at major levels.
- **Funding Squeeze**: counter-trading extreme funding positioning.
- **Liquidation Cascade Reversal**: buying the flush / shorting the euphoric spike.
- **Failed Breakout**: trading the reversal of a failed breakout attempt.
