# Recommended Reading — The Foundation

This is the canon. Read these in order, or at least skim them. Claude will reference these frameworks when producing briefs.

## Tier 1 — Non-Negotiable (read these first)

1. **Trading in the Zone** — Mark Douglas
   - The single most-recommended trading psychology book. Teaches probability-based thinking, the 5 fundamental truths, and how to execute without emotional attachment.
   - Core idea: you can have a winning edge and still lose if your mindset is built around needing to be right.

2. **The Disciplined Trader** — Mark Douglas
   - Douglas's earlier work, focused on mental habits. Pairs with Trading in the Zone.

3. **Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets** — John J. Murphy
   - The bible of technical analysis. Reference text, not a cover-to-cover read. Covers candlesticks, Fibonacci, moving averages, indicators.

4. **Reading Price Charts Bar by Bar** — Al Brooks
   - Pure price action. Teaches you to read what the market is doing *without* indicators.

## Tier 2 — Crypto-Specific Context

5. **The Bitcoin Standard** — Saifedean Ammous
   - For macro and cycle understanding. Not a trading book, but essential for understanding why Bitcoin's 4-year cycle works the way it does.

6. **An Altcoin Trader's Handbook** — Nik Patel
   - Practical crypto altcoin-focused frameworks, including spotting pumps and rotations.

7. **The Crypto Trader** — Glen Goodman
   - Good introduction for crypto-specific mechanics (exchanges, wallets, execution).

## Tier 3 — Advanced / Method-Specific

8. **Way of the Turtle** — Curtis Faith
   - The famous "Turtle Traders" rules. Systematic trend-following, position sizing, pyramiding.

9. **The New Market Wizards** — Jack Schwager
   - Interviews with legendary traders. Patterns of thinking that separate consistent winners.

10. **Best Loser Wins** — Tom Hougaard
    - A high-stake day trader's honest take on why "thinking normally" loses. Excellent for real-time emotional challenges.

11. **The Mental Game of Trading** — Jared Tendler
    - Systematic approach to solving specific emotional problems (greed, fear, tilt, confidence).

12. **Wyckoff's Original Writings + modern guides**
    - For learning accumulation/distribution schematics. Crypto shows these patterns more clearly than equities due to whale behavior.

## Tier 4 — Quant / Systematic

13. **Algorithmic Trading of Cryptocurrencies** — Ernie Chan
    - If you want to think about the systematic side rigorously.

14. **Evidence-Based Technical Analysis** — David Aronson
    - Teaches you to test and falsify your own trading ideas.

---

## Key Truth About Books

No book will give you an edge on its own. The value is:
1. Framework vocabulary (so you can think clearly about what you're seeing)
2. Cautionary tales (so you don't make the common mistakes)
3. Mental models (so you can stay disciplined)

**The actual edge comes from:** (a) journaling your own trades, (b) reviewing them honestly, (c) developing a few setups you understand deeply, and (d) sizing appropriately.
